# Your information

This is the privacy policy for the Dockside iPhone app.

Dockside runs no server of its own. akn Systems Ltd operates no database of
users, receives no copy of what anyone does in the app, and cannot see who has
installed it. Everything below is either held on the device or sent directly
from the device to a third party named here.

| | |
|---|---|
| Publisher | akn Systems Ltd, registered in England and Wales |
| Applies to | The Dockside iPhone app |
| Contact | kabir.a@akn.me.uk |

## What the app asks for

| Item | Asked for | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Location | Optional | To centre the map and show distance to docking stations |
| Cycle hire account email and password | Only to hire | To sign in to the account you already hold with the scheme operator |
| Name, date of birth, address | No | |
| Payment or card details | No | Entered on the operator's website, never in the app |
| Contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, camera | No | |
| Advertising identifier | No | |

Declining location leaves the app fully usable. The map opens on London and
distances are not shown.

## What is stored on your device

| Item | Where | Removed by |
|------|-------|------------|
| Sign-in token and account ID | iOS Keychain | Signing out, or deleting the app |
| A random device identifier, generated on first launch | iOS Keychain | Deleting the app |
| Pinned and hidden docking stations | App settings | Deleting the app |
| The most recent release code and its docking station | App settings | Dismissing the banner, or deleting the app |
| Cached account details and journey history | App storage | Signing out, or deleting the app |
| The list of docking station locations | App storage | Deleting the app |

Your password is never stored. Your email address is never stored either. Once
sign-in succeeds, the app holds the token and nothing else identifying you.

Signing out clears the token, the account ID, and the cached account details
and journey history.

## What leaves your device

Dockside sends data to two destinations. Neither is operated by akn.

### Transport for London

| Sent | When | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Coordinates of the area of map you are looking at | While the map is open | To request docking stations nearby |
| Your IP address | With every request, unavoidably | Ordinary internet routing |

These requests carry no account, no token, and no device identifier. They are
not associated with you.

### The cycle hire scheme operator

Only after you choose to sign in.

| Sent | When | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Email address and password | Once, at sign-in | To obtain a sign-in token |
| Sign-in token and account ID | With each account request | To read your details, access period, and journey history |
| The random device identifier | With each request | Required by the operator's service |
| The docking station you selected | At the moment of hire | To release a bike |
| Your IP address | With every request, unavoidably | Ordinary internet routing |

The operator is the controller of your cycle hire account. What it does with
that account, and how long it keeps your journey history, is governed by its
own privacy policy, not this one.

## What is never collected

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Analytics | The app contains no analytics SDK |
| Advertising | The app contains no advertising and no tracking |
| Crash reporting | None is collected by akn. Apple may collect crash data if you have enabled sharing with developers, in which case Apple's terms apply |
| Location history | Coordinates are used for the request in hand and not retained |
| Anything sent to akn | The app makes no request to any akn server |

Dockside does not track you across apps or websites and asks for no permission
to do so.

## Links that leave the app

Settings and the hire screen can open the operator's website inside the app.

| Link | Destination |
|------|-------------|
| Manage account & payment | The cycle hire operator's website |
| Delete Account | The cycle hire operator's website |

Once that website opens, the operator's privacy policy applies. Dockside does
not read what you type into it and does not receive your card details.

## Your rights

akn Systems Ltd holds no personal data about Dockside users, so there is
nothing held by akn to request, correct, or erase.

| To do this | Go to |
|------------|-------|
| See or correct your account details | The operator's website |
| Obtain your journey history | The operator |
| Delete your cycle hire account | The operator's website |
| Remove everything Dockside holds | Sign out, or delete the app |

Deleting the app removes the Keychain entry, the cached account details, the
journey history, and the pinned and hidden docking stations from the device.

## Children

Dockside is not directed at children. The cycle hire scheme sets its own
minimum age, which the operator enforces on the account, not the app.

## Changes

This page is the current policy. It carries no revision history. Where this
page and the app disagree, the app is correct and the page will be corrected.
