# For volunteer tutors

Tutoring is unpaid. The site states that the service is always open to new
volunteers.

## Who can volunteer

| Criterion | What the site says |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Subject knowledge | Expertise in the subject you intend to teach |
| Maths | Every tutor teaches maths and has a strong grounding in it, whatever their main subject |
| Languages | English tutors are described as native English speakers |
| Selection basis | Qualifications, experience, and commitment to helping disadvantaged students |
| Formal teaching qualification | Not stated as a requirement |
| Minimum age | Not stated |
| Minimum time commitment | Not stated |

## Applying

There is no separate volunteer form. Volunteers use the general contact form on
the home page.

1. Open https://tpi.akn.org.uk.
2. Scroll to **Get in Touch**, or select **Contact** in the navigation.
3. Enter your name in **Name**.
4. Enter your email address in **Email**.
5. In **Message**, state that you are applying to tutor, the subjects you can
   teach, and your relevant experience.
6. Select **Submit**.

All three fields on this form are required.

Alternatively, email pathway.projects@akn.org.uk with the same information.

> Do not use the form on the **Apply** page. That form is for students
> requesting tutoring.

## What happens after you apply

| Stage | What the site states |
|-------|----------------------|
| Acknowledgement | Not stated. No timeframe is published |
| Assessment | Applicants are selected on qualifications, experience, and commitment |
| Checks | Background checks are carried out on all tutors. The type of check is not named |
| Onboarding | Not described on the site |
| Publication | Current tutors are listed by name and subject on the **About** page |

## What is expected

| Expectation | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Session format | Online video call |
| Session length | 40 minutes as standard |
| Frequency | Students are advised to take at least one session per week |
| Availability | After school, weekends, and school holidays |
| Teaching approach | An individual plan per student, matched to their pace and needs |
| Materials | Study materials are supplied by the service |
| Student allocation | The service matches you to students. Students do not select tutors |
| Cancellations | Students are told to notify their tutor directly |

## Safeguarding

The site states that policies exist to protect students, that background checks
are carried out on all tutors, and that sessions run on secure online
platforms. No policy document, named safeguarding lead, reporting route, or
check type is published.

The privacy notice states that personal information is shared with
organisations the service needs to share it with for safeguarding reasons, and
with emergency services where necessary.
