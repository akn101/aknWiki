# For students and parents

Tutoring is free. There is no account to create and nothing to pay at any
point.

## Eligibility

| Question | What the site says |
|----------|--------------------|
| Who the service is for | Students from disadvantaged backgrounds who need help with their studies |
| What proof is required | Basic information about your background may be requested. The site states this is kept confidential |
| Age range | Not stated |
| School year or qualification level | Not stated |
| Location | Not stated |

Ask by email if you are unsure whether you qualify.

## Applying

The application form is on the **Apply** page.

1. Open https://tpi.akn.org.uk and select **Apply**.
2. Enter your name in **Name**. This is the only field the form requires.
3. Enter your email address in **Email**.
4. Tick each subject you want help with under **Subjects**.
5. Describe what you need in **Message**.
6. Select **Submit**.

> Only **Name** is required. The form will submit without an email address.
> The service then has no way to reply. Always fill in **Email**.

No confirmation email is sent by the service. No copy of your application is
kept anywhere you can see it.

### Applying by email

Email pathway.projects@akn.org.uk instead of using the form. Include the
student's name, the subjects wanted, and a way to reply.

## What happens next

| Stage | What the site states |
|-------|----------------------|
| Acknowledgement | Not stated. No timeframe is published |
| Contact | A tutor gets in touch to discuss your needs |
| Tutor allocation | The service matches students to tutors by academic need and personal preference. You cannot choose a tutor |
| Eligibility check | Basic background information may be requested at this stage |
| Application status | Not exposed. There is no page to check and no reference number |

If you receive no reply, send a follow-up to pathway.projects@akn.org.uk. The
form cannot be resent with a correction, and a second submission arrives as a
new application.

## Sessions

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Delivery | Online, by video call |
| Length | 40 minutes as standard. Adjustable to individual needs |
| Frequency | At least one session per week is recommended. No limit is stated |
| Timing | After school, weekends, and school holidays |
| Materials | Study materials and resources are provided for sessions and for independent study |
| Extras | Workshops on study skills and exam preparation run occasionally |
| Video platform | Not named on the site |

### What you need

- A computer or mobile device with an internet connection.
- A webcam.
- A microphone.

### Cancelling or rescheduling

Tell your tutor as soon as possible. There is no cancellation form and no
notice period is stated.

## Cost

The service is free of charge. The site asks for no payment details at any
point. Any request for payment presented as coming from the service should be
treated as suspect and reported to pathway.projects@akn.org.uk.
